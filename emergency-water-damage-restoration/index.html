How to Tell You Need Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Water is still flowing and you cannot find or reach the shutoff valve.
  • Part of your ceiling bows downward because water is pooling on top of it.
  • Gallons keep spilling from a ruptured water heater tank.
  • Pooled water is creeping over the floor into neighboring rooms or onto the staircase.
  • Sewage has backed up, or the water is dark with a foul odor.
  • Carpet, drywall or hardwood has been soaked for more than a few hours.
  • You see water escaping around a light fitting, from a wall outlet or through the ceiling.

Emergency Water Damage Restoration Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Keep the number of an emergency restoration company saved in your phone before you need it.
  • Insulate pipes in unheated spaces so they do not freeze and split in cold weather.
  • Aging supply lines to toilets, faucets and washing machines should be swapped out before one fails.
  • Place leak sensors near the water heater, washing machine, dishwasher and under sinks.
  • Important paperwork and valuables belong above floor level, most of all on the lowest story.
  • Being able to find the electrical panel quickly means wet areas can be de-energized without risk.

Our Emergency Water Damage Restoration Process

We follow a proven process to ensure every job is completed to the highest standards. Here's how we handle emergency water damage restoration from start to finish:

1

Answer the Call

Day or night, someone picks up, finds out where the water started, which rooms are hit and what hazards exist, then gives you steps to follow until the crew arrives.

2

Shut Off the Water

On arrival we check for electrical and contamination hazards, then shut off or isolate whatever is feeding the water. Every later step depends on stopping that water first.

3

Remove Standing Water

Truck-mounted extractors and pumps take up the standing water while we clear furniture from wet rooms and use meters and thermal imaging to chart moisture hidden in floors and walls.

Before leaving, we place dehumidifiers and air movers. Afterward our team explains what turned up, what to expect during the 3 to 5 days of drying ahead, and which records your insurance company needs.
